OUR MISSION

Food Can Change Lives

A smarter way to connect surplus food with people in need.

FoodRescue AI is a smart food redistribution platform designed to reduce food waste and connect food donors with NGOs and communities in need. Donors can list surplus food, NGOs can view and accept available donations, and our rule-based matching system recommends suitable NGOs using food requirements and serving capacity.
